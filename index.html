Runbook

Encrypted Backup & Retention Policy Runbook

Runbook

Standardized backup procedures specifying AES-256 encrypted snapshot creation, offsite sync channels, and automated lifecycle retention schedules for persistent volume data.

  • Encryption standard: Client-side AES-256 GPG encryption prior to transport.
